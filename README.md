# Enigma2 Player

**Website & Downloads: https://madoe21.github.io/enigma2_player/**

Enigma2 Player is a cross-platform app to **browse, control and stream your
Enigma2 satellite/cable receiver** (OpenWebIF) — live TV, radio and
recordings on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android (incl. TV boxes / Fire TV) and
iOS, with one consistent UI in Deutsch, English and Español.

## Highlights

- **Live TV & radio streaming** straight from the receiver, with channel
  lists, bouquets and EPG.
- **Recordings**: browse, play and manage what's on the box.
- **Remote control**: timers (incl. AutoTimer), receiver settings, teletext,
  bouquet editing — no need to sit in front of the TV.
- **Multi-device**: one account, licenses per device, managed self-service.
